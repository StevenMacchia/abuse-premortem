# Abuse risk catalog

58 ways products get misused, in 14 areas. The pre-mortem scores each one for a specific product, using its features, audience and markets. Severity here is the inherent harm if the risk happens; the tool adjusts likelihood for each product.

## Child safety

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)** | Critical | Imagery of child sexual abuse is uploaded, stored or traded. Any service that hosts images or video will be targeted, whatever its intended audience. |
| **Grooming and online enticement** | Critical | Adults build trust with children in private, then push for images, meetings or a move to another app. |
| **Children exposed to age-inappropriate content** | High | Young users see sexual, violent or otherwise mature content, often pushed to them by recommendations or generated on request. |
| **Under-age users on an adult-only service** | High | Self-declared age is easy to lie about. Children end up in spaces designed for adults. |
| **Unlawful collection or use of children's data** | High | Children's personal data is collected, profiled or shared without the consent and protections the law requires. |
| **Manipulative design and compulsive use by young users** | Medium | Streaks, endless feeds and spending mechanics exploit young users' developing self-control. |

## Sexual exploitation

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Sextortion** | High | Victims are pressured into sharing intimate images, then blackmailed for money or more images. Organized gangs run this at scale. |
| **Intimate images shared without consent** | High | Intimate images, including sexual deepfakes, are shared without the consent of the person shown. |
| **Sex trafficking and commercial sexual exploitation** | Critical | People, including minors, are advertised, recruited or coerced into commercial sex through the platform. |
| **Unsolicited sexual images and messages** | Medium | People receive explicit images or sexual messages they didn't ask for. |

## Fraud and scams

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Scams targeting users** | High | Romance, investment, fake job and fake support scams. Scammers build trust, then ask for money, often moving victims off the platform. |
| **Authorised push payment (APP) fraud** | High | Customers are tricked into sending money themselves, for example to someone posing as their bank, a seller or a romantic partner. |
| **Non-delivery, counterfeit and misrepresented goods or services** | Medium | Buyers pay and receive nothing, receive fakes, or get something different from what was listed. |
| **Stolen cards and payment fraud** | Medium | Stolen payment details are used to buy things or move money, leaving you with chargebacks, fees and processor penalties. |
| **Promotion, referral and incentive abuse** | Low | Fake accounts farm sign-up bonuses, referral rewards and promo codes. |
| **Impersonation of people, brands or your staff** | Medium | Accounts pose as real people, popular creators, brands or your own support team to deceive users. |
| **Scam, prohibited and discriminatory ads** | High | Self-serve ads promote scams, prohibited products, or target people in discriminatory ways. |

## Financial crime

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Money laundering and terrorist financing** | Critical | Criminal proceeds are moved and disguised through accounts, transactions, stored value or items on your platform. |
| **Money mules and rented accounts** | High | Criminals recruit people, often young, to receive and pass on stolen money, or rent their verified accounts. |
| **Sanctions violations** | High | Money or services reach sanctioned people, organizations or countries. |
| **Financial harm to vulnerable customers** | High | Customers in difficult circumstances take on unaffordable debt, lose savings to speculation or are financially abused by others. |

## Account security

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Account takeover** | Medium | Attackers take over accounts through stolen passwords, phishing, SIM swaps or weak recovery flows. |
| **Malware and phishing** | Medium | Users are sent malicious files or phishing links through your product. |

## Harassment and hate

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Harassment, bullying and pile-ons** | Medium | Individuals are targeted with abuse, threats or coordinated pile-ons. |
| **Hate speech** | Medium | Content that attacks people for protected characteristics such as race, religion, gender, sexuality or disability. |
| **Discrimination by hosts, providers or algorithms** | Medium | People are refused service, charged more or shown different opportunities because of who they are. |

## Violent and extremist content

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Terrorist and violent extremist content** | Critical | Propaganda, recruitment and attack footage from terrorist and violent extremist groups. |
| **Graphic violence and gore** | Medium | Shocking imagery reaches people who didn't choose to see it. |
| **Credible threats of violence** | High | Threats against individuals, schools or places that may be carried out. |

## Self-harm and wellbeing

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Suicide, self-harm and eating-disorder content** | Critical | Content or conversations that encourage, instruct or glamorize suicide, self-harm or disordered eating. |
| **Dangerous challenges and viral stunts** | High | Trends that encourage young people to do risky things on camera. |
| **Harmful health misinformation** | High | False medical claims, dangerous treatments or bad advice presented as fact. |

## Privacy and surveillance

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Doxxing and exposure of personal information** | High | Someone's home address, phone number, workplace or other private details are published to target them. |
| **Scraping and bulk harvesting of user data** | Medium | Bots collect profiles and content at scale for spam, fraud, surveillance or resale. |
| **Staff or contractor misuse of user data** | High | People with internal access look up, leak or misuse private data. |
| **Hidden cameras and privacy violations in rentals** | High | Guests are recorded without their knowledge. |

## Physical safety

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Stalking and location tracking** | Critical | Someone's location is used to follow, find or harm them. |
| **Assault or harm during in-person meetings** | Critical | Physical or sexual violence when people meet, visit or provide services face to face. |
| **Harm to workers and service providers** | High | Drivers, couriers, hosts and freelancers face assault, harassment or unsafe situations from customers. |
| **Unvetted people working under someone else's account** | High | Verified workers rent or share their accounts, so the person who shows up hasn't been checked. |

## Platform integrity

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Fake and bot accounts at scale** | Medium | Automated or mass-created accounts used for spam, fraud, manipulation or reward farming. |
| **Spam and malicious links** | Low | Unwanted promotional content and links flood conversations and feeds. |
| **Fake and manipulated reviews** | Medium | Bought, swapped or self-written reviews mislead customers; honest reviews get suppressed. |
| **Coordinated inauthentic behavior and disinformation** | High | Networks of fake or coordinated accounts push narratives, products or political disinformation. |
| **Automation abuse through your API** | Medium | Developers or bots use your API to spam, scrape or manipulate at scale. |
| **Untraceable abuse through guest access** | Medium | Guests can't be warned, banned or held to account. Removed users come straight back, and reports lack evidence of who did what. |

## Illegal and regulated goods

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Drugs, weapons and other prohibited goods** | High | Sale or promotion of illegal drugs, weapons, counterfeit documents and other prohibited items. |
| **Counterfeits and copyright infringement** | Medium | Counterfeit goods and pirated content expose you to rights-holder claims. |
| **Gambling and gambling-like mechanics** | Medium | Loot boxes, skin betting or unlicensed gambling, including by minors. |

## AI misuse

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-generated child sexual abuse material** | Critical | The model is prompted to produce sexualized imagery or text involving children. |
| **Deceptive or sexual deepfakes of real people** | High | Synthetic media of real people used for harassment, fraud or disinformation. |
| **Jailbreaks for dangerous information** | High | Users get around safeguards to obtain weapons, hacking or self-harm instructions. |
| **Emotional dependency and harmful AI companion responses** | High | People form intense attachments to an AI that may encourage isolation, validate harmful ideas or mishandle a crisis. |
| **Prompt injection and AI agent misuse** | Medium | Instructions hidden in content hijack the AI to leak data or take unwanted actions. |

## Operational readiness

| Risk | Severity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **No one owns urgent safety escalations** | High | A child-safety report, credible threat or live incident arrives and nobody is responsible for acting on it. |
| **Unprepared for law-enforcement and emergency requests** | Medium | Police request data or an emergency disclosure, and there is no process to verify and respond. |
| **Moderator exposure to traumatic content** | Medium | Reviewers see graphic, sexual or violent material that harms their mental health. |
| **Wrongful enforcement without redress** | Medium | Mistaken removals and bans with no explanation or appeal erode trust and breach some laws. |
